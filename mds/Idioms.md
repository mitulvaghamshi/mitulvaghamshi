# Idioms

1. Every cloud has a silver lining

- Every sad or unpleasant situation has a positive side to it.

2. Over the moon

- To be extremely pleased or happy.

3. Once in a blue moon

- Happens very rarely.

4. A piece of cake

- Very easy.

5. A drop in the ocean

- A very small part of something bigger.

6. Action speaks louder than words

- It is better to actually do something than just talk about it.

7. Back to the drawing board

- When you attempt to do something but failed and have tried again.

8. Put all your eggs in one basket

- Put all your money and effort in one thing.

9. The in thing

- Something fashionable.

10. The real McCoy

- Genuine, not fake.

11. Off the top of my head

- Saying something without thinking first.

12. Run of the mill

- Average, ordinary.

13. Soulmate

- Someone you trust very deeply.

14. Down in the dumps

- Sad.

15. Found my feet

- To become comfortable doing something.

16. Set in their ways

- Not wanting to change.

17. Go the extra mile

- Too much more than is required.

18. A hot potato

- A controversial topic.

19. Miss the boat

- Miss an opportunity.

20. Cost an arm and a leg

- Really expensive.

21. Sit on the fence

- To be undecided.
